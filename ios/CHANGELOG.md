# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Contract revision 39 (`schedule:cards`, provisional): when the paired Mac offers it, the iPhone asks for it in a call of its own after its other opt-ins, and a scheduled-task approval becomes a full review card instead of a one-line summary. The card asks what approving would do ("Save this scheduled task?", "Pause this scheduled task?", "Run this scheduled task now?"), leads with the task's name, cadence and next run (read in the phone's own time zone and clock), then lists where it runs (the project, its connectors, "Anywhere on your computer" for Full access, or "No project"), its access as a soft Read-only or warning-tinted Full access chip, the model or "Script", notifications and time zone. The instructions or script follow in a block that folds to six lines behind **Show all**, with a note when the Mac shortened them, and a note says when scheduling is off or the task stays paused. A card that fails the phone's checks is dropped and never changes what the approval allows; older Macs, and the Assistant's automation edits, keep the summary card.
- The approval card matches the desktop redesign: an accent icon tile, the action as a plain question ("Run this command?", "Edit forecast.ts?"), the exact command or path in a code block without the "Run command:" prefix (long requests fold to six lines behind **Show all** and scroll in a bounded box once expanded, with a **Scroll for more** cue while the end is out of view), a centered "Aiden is paused until you choose." note, and two full-width buttons. **Allow once** carries a chevron menu for **Allow for this chat** and **Always allow** when the Mac offers them, each saying what it covers: the exact command, or any later edit to that file. An action that only the Mac may allow now says "Allow this on your Mac. You can deny it here." A scheduled task asks "Save this scheduled task?" (or "Change this automation?"), as on the Mac, and the card's shadow no longer blurs its text in light mode. A chat's activity summary now says "Skipped 1 command" for a denied call instead of "Ran 1 command", and "Waiting for approval" while a call waits, matching the Mac.
- Contract revision 37 (`bot:handoffs`): when the paired Mac offers it, the iPhone asks for it in a call of its own. A Bot's chat then shows the step the Bot hands back ("Your turn: …") next to the question and approval cards. **Done**, **Skip** and **Open** answer it, and a note typed in the composer is sent as Done. A sign-in offers **Finish on your Mac** and **Skip**. The Bot profile's More menu offers **Pause Bot** and **Resume Bot**, and a paused Bot's chat shows a Resume card and holds the composer. Bots list rows now read "Interrupted — tap to resume" for an interrupted Bot and "Paused" for one you paused. A hand-off that arrives while the app is in the background posts one notification that opens the chat. Older Macs keep the "needs you on your Mac" message.
- Smoother chat streaming. A finished reply no longer flashes twice or jumps as it settles: the live reply is replaced by the saved one in the same frame, a reply that only ran tools stays on screen until the saved transcript arrives, and "Working for" now sits in the footer row that "Worked for" takes over. Text that arrives in bursts is paced out word by word over about a quarter second instead of landing in lumps (never more than ~600 characters behind, and the end of a reply drains in instead of dumping; off under Reduce Motion), in workspace and Bot chats. Half-received Markdown no longer flashes as raw syntax: an open `**bold`, `` `code`` or link on the line being written draws finished, and a lone list or heading marker waits for its text. A sent message keeps its row when the Mac confirms it, so its attachment thumbnails no longer reload.
- Contract revision 36: a chat whose unseen output is a failed turn now says **Failed** (a soft red pill, VoiceOver "Failed") in place of the unread dot, so a run that failed in the background no longer looks like a fresh reply. Opening the chat clears it with the dot. Older Macs omit the hint and keep the dot.
- Home opens from what this iPhone saved: the workspace list is now kept per paired Mac, so Recents and Workspaces show at once while the Mac connects instead of "No Chats Yet" behind a "Connecting to Aiden Agent…" overlay. A first read with nothing saved shows shaped skeleton rows, empty states appear only after the Mac answers, and an unreachable Mac with nothing saved says so. Connecting is a quiet one-line hint, Scheduled Tasks and Usage are no longer greyed out while connecting, and New chat waits for the connection instead of being disabled. Recents rows with a saved transcript open immediately and refresh once the Mac connects, and the five newest transcripts are saved in the background after each refresh (skipped in Low Data Mode) without changing their unread or needs-approval state. A failed Usage read says so with Try Again instead of loading forever.
- Contract revision 35 (`chat-visual-viewer-v1`): when the paired Mac announces it, tapping a chart or widget in a reply opens it full screen and interactive in the phone's theme and light or dark scheme, in a locked-down web view that keeps nothing on disk and follows no links. A follow-up the visual suggests is only added to the message after **Add to message**; links ask before opening. The last copy of each opened visual stays available offline, marked as possibly out of date, and the snapshot's image viewer is still one tap away. Older Macs keep today's image viewer.

- The three onboarding pages now draw their art natively: tinted, cropped slices of the workspace chat, the model menu, and Scheduled Tasks that follow light, dark, and every theme preset, with motion that holds still under Reduce Motion and Low Power Mode. The old clay images are gone.

- Agent details now follow the delegated-agent ancestry: Back returns through parents to the roster, and Started by / Sub-agents links stay inside the current chat inspector.

- An attachment upload whose cleanup finds the device revoked now finishes only after the installation data has been purged, even when chat removal started the revocation.
- Tool calls whose arguments are still streaming now read "Preparing <tool>" in activity rows, matching Aiden Agent, instead of claiming the work already started.
