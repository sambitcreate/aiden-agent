**Proactive Aiden: chats that check back, keep going, and run other chats.**
- Use `/goal` (or `/until`) to keep a chat iterating toward a finish condition with reviewer rounds, keep-awake support, and a live goal bar.
- Chats can schedule check-backs, propose and run event-triggered tasks (watches, links, and connectors), and orchestrate other chats (`child_chats`) in the same workspace or new worktrees.
- Follow-through chips and pull-request watching keep tracking an open PR until reviews, CI, and merge conditions are done.

**Floating Quick Chat and faster worktrees.**
- Summon the Floating Quick Chat starter from anywhere with the global hotkey, expand into the mini-modal, or attach an ephemeral quick chat into a full workspace thread.
- Worktree creation and worktree-on-send are significantly faster across Git, the main process, `.worktreeinclude` copying, and frame-1 draft handoff.

**iOS companion: iPhone Duo, Home Screen widget, and pairing polish.**
- Regular-width iPhones and split views gain the side-by-side Duo layout with fold continuity across drafts, reading position, and Search, plus quieter card lists and the ink Aiden accent.
- Add the **Ask Aiden** Home Screen widget and Quick Actions to jump straight into a chat or camera capture, alongside smoother QR pairing and unpaired Search follow-ups.

**Bots, desktop UI, and reliability.**
- Bots share one capability snapshot per turn, offer **Always allow** on their first permission card, stop showing stale scheduling notes, and drop `"Sending…"` noise on phones.
- Theme tiles preview in the active light or dark mode, busy turns keep the live transcript steady without jumping, narrow composers close the empty side lane, and Scheduled tasks drop decorative sparkle icons.
