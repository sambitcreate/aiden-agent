Why these are cheaper than the orbs

Today: thinking-orbs

  • Each orb is a <canvas> redrawn every frame from a requestAnimationFrame loop. JS projects a 3D dotted shape, repaints the whole canvas, and re-uploads it to the GPU.
  • That runs at the display rate, up to 120 Hz on ProMotion. Several run at once: the activity row, each subagent chip, and up to four 64px canvases in Live.

These marks

  • Inline SVG with 11 shapes or fewer, drawn in currentColor. Only transform and opacity are keyframed, with no filters, blur or gradients.
  • They pause off-screen. Stepped timing cuts updates to about 4 per segment. Reduced Motion shows a visible still pose.
  • Chromium may still repaint the small SVG each frame, but at 20px with no JS that cost is small.
