# Telegram durable Bot ingress: commands, buttons, Stop & album retries (#534, #535) — 2026-11-18

Branch: `fix/telegram-bot-ingress-534-535`. Fixes GitHub issues `#534` and `#535`.

## Issue #534: Durable Bot session routing for Bot-bound commands, callbacks & controls

- **Boundary & defensive guard (`main/services/telegram/telegram-service-core.ts`):** When a Telegram route resolves to a Bot binding and `botIngress` is wired (`isDurableBotRoute`), prompts are never admitted to `enqueuePrompt` or executed by `dispatchTurn` against `binding.backingChatId`. Both `enqueuePrompt` and `dispatchTurn` carry defensive guards throwing if a durable Bot turn ever reaches the legacy queue path.
- **Supported durable admissions (`main/services/telegram/bot-reply-outbox.ts`, `telegram-service-core.ts`):**
  - `/queue <text>` and `/continue [text]` (default `"Continue."`) admit through `admitBotTurn` with `whenBusy: "followUp"` (forwarded to `BotSessionService.send`) and the real incoming `message.message_id`.
  - Expanded prompt-template commands (`template.expand`) admit their expanded prompt through `admitBotTurn` with `whenBusy: "followUp"` and real `message.message_id` (removing any `sourceMessageId ?? 0` fallback). Edited prompt-producing commands (`/queue`, `/continue`, `/interrupt`, and prompt-producing templates) are explicitly rejected rather than re-admitted. Direct extension command handlers (`template.handle`) remain unchanged.
  - `tgbtn:` inline button callbacks admit through `admitBotTurn` with `whenBusy: "followUp"` and callback-query-scoped deduplication identity `tg:<botId>:<chatId>:<threadId>:cbq:<callbackQueryId>`, preserving `tg:` provenance while avoiding collisions with numeric message IDs.
- **Replay-safe button store & pending receipts (`main/services/telegram/telegram-outbound.ts`, `telegram-service.ts`):**
  - `createTelegramButtonStore` mints CSPRNG `tgbtn:<base64url>` tokens (non-reused across restarts), scopes actions to `(profile, chatId, threadId, ownerUserId, messageId, bindingKey)`, and persists actions, pending callback receipts, and Stop target fences in `<userData>/telegram-bot-outbox/<profile>/buttons.json`.
  - `prepareCallback` stages a durable receipt before calling `admitBotTurn` without consuming the button yet, and `tgbtn:` admission runs outside the generic `handleCallback` catch so transient failures and `BotReplyAdmittedUnrecordedError` reach `runPollLoop` backoff/retry and recover across restarts. Selection styling (`editMessageReplyMarkup`) and `answerCallbackQuery` after durable acceptance are best-effort. Unknown, expired, cross-thread, or rebound tokens fail closed (`"This action has expired."`).
- **Fenced durable Bot Stop & truthful menus (`main/services/bot-runtime/bot-session-service.ts`, `telegram-controls.ts`, `telegram-service.ts`):**
  - `BotSessionService.stop(botId, { expectedSubmissionId })` fences Stop inside per-Bot serialization so a stale target or update replay cannot abort a successor submission. `telegram-service.ts` wraps Stop with `stopWithIntents` (rolling back with `stopCancelled` when a successor submission was spared).
  - `/stop`, `/abort`, `/cancel`, `turn:stop[:<submissionId>]`, and `turn:abort[:<submissionId>]` on durable Bot routes invoke `botSession.stop` (never `abortChat(backingChatId)`), persist the intended `targetSubmissionId` per command/callback identity across replays, and report truthfully that queued follow-ups are withdrawn.
  - Bound `/status`, `/start`, `/queue`, and `/help` reflect durable `botSession.state` (`working`, `paused`, `interrupted`, etc.), offer a single submission-fenced `turn:stop:<submissionId>` control while running, and explain durable follow-ups instead of showing an empty legacy queue.
- **Intentional unsupported operations on durable Bot routes:** `/interrupt`, `/next` (`turn:next`), `/steer`, per-item queue editing callbacks (`queue:item:*`, `queue:priority:*`, `queue:delete:*`, `queue:clear:*`), and explicit skill invocations (`template.skillInvocation`, which `BotSendInput` cannot represent safely) reply with explicit unavailable messages rather than falling back to `backingChatId`. Unbound Telegram chats remain unchanged.

## Issue #535: Bounded retry & sticky outbox recovery for Bot-bound Telegram albums

- **Tracked debounced album retry (`main/services/telegram/telegram-service-core.ts`):**
  - `scheduleMediaGroup` hands flushed Bot-bound media groups to `startBotMediaGroupAdmission`, holding the frozen assembled turn (without re-downloading or re-merging attachments) in `retryingMediaGroups` and keeping it counted in `checkPendingCapacity` until settled.
  - Ordinary transient failures retry up to 5 attempts with exponential backoff (`3s`, `6s`, `12s`, `24s`, capped at `60s`) via `deps.sleep(pause, controller.signal)` and revalidate the captured binding on each attempt. Terminal `BotSessionError` reasons (`needs_model`, `model_error`, `unavailable`, `access_changed`, `bot_missing`, `bot_deleted`) stop immediately on attempt 1. One `⚠️ Error:` message is sent to the chat/topic only upon terminal failure or retry exhaustion.
  - `BotReplyAdmittedUnrecordedError` marks `admitted = true` stickily on the retry task so an already-accepted Bot album continues retrying past the 5-attempt cutoff (with capped 60s backoff, deduplicated via `tg:<botId>:<chatId>:<threadId>:<firstMessageId>`) even if subsequent attempts fail with generic outbox read/storage errors.
  - Service `stop()`, `disconnect()`, and `resetPairing()` abort in-flight retry waits and prevent old tasks from scheduling further attempts or sending late error messages after restart.
- **Intentional restart limitation:** Because Telegram offsets advance as each album item is buffered into memory during debounce, buffered or retrying un-admitted albums remain process-lifetime state and are not crash-durable across a full process stop/restart (whereas an album already accepted by `BotSessionService.send` before crash remains in the Bot's durable session).
