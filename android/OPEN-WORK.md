# Open work on Android

On a host offering the negotiated `bot:open-work` capability (Remote revision 55), open **Bot profile → Open work**. Reading task text and owned evidence requires both Bot-read and chat-read visibility. Changes additionally need Bot-edit permission. Older hosts hide the profile entry; a restored destination explains the missing host support or visibility. No new feature token is used.

Add a title and note to record work without starting or scheduling the Bot. Edit an item to choose Open, Waiting, Done or Dismissed; Waiting requires a reason. Done/Reopen/Dismiss are also available directly on the item. Completion says whether a person marked it done or the Bot completed it. Execution status is separate and comes from the host, including changes at an unchanged record revision.

**Undo last change** uses the host's per-item Undo option for up to 24 hours. A later edit invalidates it. Undo is a new revision-checked, idempotent operation; disappearance of an Undo option never proves an unconfirmed Undo succeeded.

**Stop linked work** and **Stop and dismiss** appear only when the host says this exact execution can be stopped. A mixed-input execution explains why it cannot be stopped from the item. An item with a requested/uncertain Stop or stopping execution is read-only even when another device requested it; edits (including an already-open draft), Done, Dismiss and Undo wait for the host acknowledgement without creating a new mutation or journal attempt. Requested or uncertain Stop remains unfinished and blocks further changes until acknowledgement. **Check Stop** reuses the same operation identity and exact payload, including after ordinary receipt retention expires. A matching terminal Stop receipt can acknowledge stopped/already-finished/not-running or a refusal; a blocked receipt says Stop was refused and never claims the task stopped. Dismiss alone changes the record without undoing or cancelling work already admitted.

**View source** and **View result** load the actual retained text through the owning item's evidence route. The phone cannot author transcript IDs, paths, provenance or execution links. The sheet says when evidence is missing or truncated. Evidence is bounded to 32 KiB of UTF-8 text and a 256 KiB response, shown as plain text and not persisted in the request journal. Source/result submission and entry IDs stay decimal strings without numeric conversion.

The resumed screen refreshes the host's saved projection every 15 seconds. It stops on pause or disposal and never executes work, tests a connector or resends a mutation. Local drafts and frozen requests are independent of refresh. Text entry obeys the host's scalar and UTF-8 byte budgets and rejects NUL/unpaired surrogates; clipping preserves complete characters.

Before any mutation, the phone synchronously commits its exact UUID, payload and original start time to the existing encrypted, backup-excluded preference store, scoped to host/device/pairing/Bot in a separate Open-work namespace. Startup restores without automatically retrying. Concurrent controller/window updates use compare-and-replace, so a stale window cannot overwrite another window's frozen request; a conflict refuses transmission and asks the person to reopen the app. Forgetting or replacing the pairing purges its journal and fences late callbacks. A secure-storage failure refuses transmission rather than dropping an unknown identity.

Unknown transport outcomes and even a first typed `unavailable` response remain frozen because publication may have committed. Later replay errors, including permission loss, stale revisions or missing resources, cannot replace that identity. Only a successful same-key response or positive host evidence settles it: a matching Undo mutation nonce for add/edit/dismiss, or the exact terminal Stop nonce. No settlement uses matching titles, content or timestamps. Ordinary unknown requests stop replay after a conservative 23-hour window and remain available for authoritative refresh; protected pending Stop checks continue with their original identity. A stale first save preserves the draft until the person reviews the host record and explicitly chooses its latest revision.

JVM coverage is in `AidenBotOpenWorkContractTest`, `AidenBotOpenWorkClientTest` and `features.bots.AidenBotOpenWorkControllerTest`, plus journal/pairing and navigation suites. `AidenBotOpenWorkEditorUiTest` covers waiting reasons, Unicode entry and read-only controls. The normative fixture is `protocol/aiden-remote/v1/fixtures/bot-open-work.json`. Compilation, JVM tests and lint do not constitute paired-device, TalkBack or visual acceptance.

Revision57 adds host-owned peer sources and exchange links when `bot:peers` is negotiated. **View exchange** opens the same Bot’s peer detail; linked Stop and source/result evidence remain owned and authorized by the host’s Open-work routes. Older audiences receive the host’s compatibility projection.
