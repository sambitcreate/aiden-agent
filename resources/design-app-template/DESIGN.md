## Overview

A neutral starting point for designing in this folder. Replace these tokens with the product's own as the design takes shape.

## Colors

Background and surface carry the page; text and muted carry words; primary marks the one action that matters on a screen. Each colour has a dark value.

## Typography

One system font stack. Heading for page titles, body for reading text, small for captions and labels, mono for code and numbers.

## Layout

Spacing steps of 4, 8, 16, 24 and 40 pixels. Keep content in one column up to 720 pixels wide.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat surfaces. Separate areas with the surface colour rather than shadows.

## Shapes

Small radius for inputs and chips, medium for buttons and cards, large for sheets.

## Components

Buttons use the primary colour for the primary action and the surface colour for the rest.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do use the tokens above. Don't add one-off colours or font sizes.
